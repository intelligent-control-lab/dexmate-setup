B10

Gripper hardware setup

One-time setup

1. Connect the assembled harness

The assembled harness has a CAN adapter at one end and a 4-pin / 6-pin pair on each of its two branches. Connect each 6-pin plug to the gripper and each 4-pin plug to the robot.

2. Connect the wrist camera cable

Connect the camera cable as well. Use only the connector on the inward-facing side, as shown in IMG_9360.

3. Connect USB and set both switches

Plug the CAN adapter into a robot USB port. Both switches must be toward the K side, as shown in IMG_9369.

B11

PCB and camera hardware setup

One-time setup

Reference: Waveshare MAX9296-GMSL-Deser-Module · Jetson Orin. Power off before changing connections.

1. Mount the capture board and connect power

Use the brass standoffs supplied in the kit to connect the capture board and PCB, then connect the power cable. Connect the red wires to header pins 2 and 4 (5 V), and ground to pin 6 (GND). These Jetson header pins are 5 V; the grippers' 24 V supply must not be connected here.

2. Set the CFG switches to 0100

Read the switches in their numbered order 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 and set them to 0 → 1 → 0 → 0.

3. Replace the robot's standoffs

Remove and keep the robot's original brass standoffs. First secure the steel frame with 15 mm standoffs, then add 10 mm standoffs to mount the remaining memory/storage assembly and fan.

4. Connect the internal wrist camera cables

  • Female–female cable: disconnect the existing internal wrist-camera connection and connect it to the capture board.
  • Male–female cable: remove the external-camera connector from the robot's rear cover and connect directly with the male–female cable. IMG_9277 demonstrates this connection.

5. Replace the CAM1 ribbon cable

Disconnect the cable originally attached to Jetson CAM1. Use the ribbon cable supplied in the kit to connect Jetson CAM1 ↔ capture board CSI0.

Keep every part removed from the robot, including the original standoffs, connectors and cables. They will be needed when the robot is returned.
