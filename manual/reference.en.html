Depth back-projection

Optical camera coordinates · metres
X = (u - cx) * Z / fx
Y = (v - cy) * Z / fy

The two supplied URDFs match dexmate_urdf 0.8.4 byte-for-byte. Their original bytes and relative mesh paths are retained. Meshes resolve within the package's directory tree; the URDF downloads alone do not include the meshes. The custom comparison model is a separate file.

Coordinate convention

T_parent_child maps column-vector coordinates from child to parent: p_parent = T_parent_child @ p_child. Translations are in metres; angles in radians. R = Rz(yaw) @ Ry(pitch) @ Rx(roll). Base axes: X forward, Y robot-left, Z up. The calibrated left-camera optical axes are X image-right, Y image-down, Z forward.
